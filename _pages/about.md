A Short Bio
======

I am a Full Researcher (equivalent to Full Professor in the U.S. academic system) and a Ph.D. advisor at Shandong University. I am a recipient of the Qilu Outstanding Young Scholar Award. Before joining Shandong University, I was a Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of California, Santa Barbara (2023–2025), and I interned at Meta Zurich in 2022. I received my Ph.D. from Shandong University, China (2017–2022).

Research
======
My research focuses on the intersection of artificial intelligence (AI) and photorealistic rendering, particularly on visual appearance modeling (as applied in the metaverse and digital human contexts). I am committed to unraveling the intricacies of why the real world appears as it does and how we can model interactions with light in an accurate and effective manner. Two distinct rendering paradigms have emerged: one focused on studying optical phenomena and physical structures that influence appearance and building models that adhere to physical laws, and the other on integrating data-driven approaches—such as training neural networks on observational data—to enhance the accuracy and realism of digital representations. Ultimately, my goal is to combine these two approaches to recreate the subtle nuances of the real world within digital environments.  Specifically, my work focuses on:
- ⭐ **Appearance Modeling**: Developed high-fidelity models for cloth, hair/fur, skin, feathers, and glint, with real-world deployment in production pipelines at Meta and Weta (*Avatar: The Way of Water*, cloth), as well as Weta and Tencent (*Cocaine Bear*, hair/fur)—targeting ultimate photorealism across both human and natural appearances.
- **Frame Extrapolation**: Real-time frame rate enhancement techniques optimized for PC and mobile platforms.
- **Global Illumination**: Real-time global illumination solutions for both PC and mobile devices.
- **Stereo Rendering**: Optimized stereo rendering algorithms bridging virtual and real-world visual experiences.
- **Appearance Aggregation with Geometry Simplification**: Accelerating rendering performance while preserving visual fidelity.
- **Inverse Rendering**: Inferring scene materials and lighting from captured imagery.
- **Simulation Rendering**: Integrating physical simulations seamlessly with rendering pipelines.
- **Scene Representation**: Efficient scene representation and reconstruction techniques.
